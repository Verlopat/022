# Ethics and Transparency Statement

The study involved no human participants and no personal data. All traffic was simulated, and the detector error profiles were set from accuracy figures published with the UVH-26 dataset, which is released under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Every controller was tuned on seeds 500-505, and its setting was frozen before the test seeds 1-20 were run. All test runs are reported, including those in which the proposed controller was slower than a baseline or a reference, the diagnostic comparison with LPS-T is reported in full, and all nine training configurations of the DQN-C baseline are listed with their search space.

# Data and Code Availability

The simulator, the controllers, the estimator and Q-network code, the trained weights, the frozen configurations and the raw logs of all tuning and test runs are available from the corresponding author and will be deposited in a public repository on acceptance. Every table and figure in this paper is produced by a script included in that package.
